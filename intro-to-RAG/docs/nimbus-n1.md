# Nimbus N1 — by Halcyon

The Nimbus N1 is Halcyon's 2024 endurance phone. The whole pitch is battery life
and reliability on a budget, with no expensive extras that drain power or raise
the price.

## Display

A 6.7-inch LCD, not OLED, at 1080 x 2400. It refreshes at a fixed 90Hz and
reaches a modest 900 nits of peak brightness. LCD is the efficiency choice — it
costs less and sips power on bright static content.

## Camera

The rear system is a 50MP main sensor with a 2MP depth sensor, and nothing else.
It does well in good light but struggles after dark. There is no ultrawide and no
telephoto. Video records at 1080p 30fps.

## Battery and charging

A 6800mAh cell delivers up to 34 hours of video playback — the largest cell in
the group. Wired charging is a deliberately slow 20W to protect long-term
battery health, taking about two hours for a full charge. There is no wireless
charging.

## Performance

Built on the efficiency-tuned Breeze 5 with 6GB of RAM, it keeps messaging,
browsing, and video smooth, but demanding games drop frames. Storage is 128GB,
expandable via microSD — the only phone here with a card slot.

## Connectivity

It supports Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.1, and dual physical SIM. The USB-C port runs
at USB 2.0 speeds.

## Software and updates

A light, near-stock Android build with an aggressive battery-saver mode that can
stretch to three days. Halcyon promises two OS upgrades and three years of
security patches.

## Price and availability

The Nimbus N1 launched at $329, in Slate and Forest. Sold online and through
prepaid carrier channels.
