# Vertex V2 — by Kestrel

The Vertex V2 is Kestrel's 2024 flagship, built for mobile gamers and
photography enthusiasts who want top-tier hardware and will pay for it.

## Display

A 6.7-inch LTPO OLED at 1440 x 3200. It refreshes adaptively between 1Hz and
120Hz and reaches 2400 nits of peak brightness. Touch sampling hits 240Hz in
game mode, and the glass is Kestrel ArmorGlass 3.

## Camera

The rear system is a 50MP main sensor with optical stabilisation, a 48MP 5x
periscope telephoto, and a 12MP ultrawide. The periscope holds detail at long
range where most phones fall apart. Night mode stacks up to 20 frames. Video
records at 8K 30fps.

## Battery and charging

A 5200mAh cell delivers up to 29 hours of video playback. Wired charging peaks
at 90W, filling the battery in about 32 minutes. Wireless charging tops out at
25W, and a bypass mode runs the phone off the cable during long sessions.

## Performance

Built on the flagship Helios G3 with 12GB of RAM, it sustains high frame rates
thanks to a large vapor-chamber cooling plate. Storage is 256GB or 1TB of fast
UFS 4.0, not expandable.

## Connectivity

It supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, ultra-wideband, and dual SIM plus eSIM. The
USB-C port runs at 10Gbps.

## Software and updates

A clean Android build with a dedicated game dashboard. Kestrel promises four OS
upgrades and five years of security patches. Extras include a performance
overlay and a recording suite.

## Price and availability

The Vertex V2 launched at $1,099, in Obsidian and Titanium. Sold unlocked
through Kestrel's store and premium retailers.
