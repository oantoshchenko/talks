# Onyx O5 — by Torch

The Onyx O5 is Torch's 2024 compact premium phone. It targets people who want a
small phone without cutting the specs down to budget level.

## Display

A 6.1-inch flat OLED at 1080 x 2340. It refreshes adaptively between 60Hz and
120Hz and reaches 2100 nits of peak brightness. The small footprint is the whole
point — it is the easiest phone here to use one-handed.

## Camera

The rear system is a 48MP main sensor with optical stabilisation and a 12MP
ultrawide. There is no telephoto — a size trade. Colour is natural rather than
punchy. Night mode stacks up to 15 frames. Video records at 4K 60fps.

## Battery and charging

A 4000mAh cell delivers up to 18 hours of video playback — the smallest cell
here, a consequence of the compact body. Wired charging peaks at 45W, filling
the battery in about 50 minutes. Wireless charging tops out at 15W.

## Performance

Built on the flagship Helios G3 with 8GB of RAM, it is as fast as the big
phones, though the small chassis throttles sooner under load. Storage is 256GB,
not expandable.

## Connectivity

It supports Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, and a single SIM plus eSIM. The USB-C port
runs at 10Gbps.

## Software and updates

A clean Android build tuned for one-handed use. Torch promises four OS upgrades
and five years of security patches.

## Price and availability

The Onyx O5 launched at $899, in Black and Sand. Sold unlocked through Torch's
store and select retailers.
