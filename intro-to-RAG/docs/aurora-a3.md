# Aurora A3 — by Lumen

The Aurora A3 is Lumen's 2024 camera-first phone. It targets people who want
great point-and-shoot photos on a mid-range budget, leaning on software rather
than expensive hardware.

## Display

A 6.5-inch flat OLED at 1080 x 2400. It refreshes adaptively between 60Hz and
120Hz and reaches 1800 nits of peak brightness. The flat frame is easy to hold
one-handed, and the panel supports HDR10+.

## Camera

The rear system is a 64MP main sensor with optical stabilisation, a 13MP
ultrawide, and a 5MP macro. The trick is software: it merges nine frames per
shot to keep noise low in dim rooms. Portrait mode separates hair from the
background. Video records at 4K 30fps.

## Battery and charging

A 4600mAh cell delivers up to 21 hours of video playback. Wired charging peaks
at 33W, filling the battery in about 75 minutes. There is no wireless charging —
a deliberate trade to keep the body thin and the price down.

## Performance

Built on the mid-range Zephyr 7 with 8GB of RAM, it handles everyday apps and
casual games smoothly, but sustained heavy gaming throttles after twenty minutes
without a vapor chamber. Storage is 128GB or 256GB, not expandable.

## Connectivity

It supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, and a single SIM plus eSIM. The USB-C port
runs at USB 2.0 speeds.

## Software and updates

A clean, near-stock Android build. Lumen promises three OS upgrades and four
years of security patches. The only extras are a pro manual mode and an
astrophotography timer.

## Price and availability

The Aurora A3 launched at $499, in Graphite, Sage, and Blush. Sold unlocked
online and through two carrier partners.
