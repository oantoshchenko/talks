# Solis S4 — by Meridian

The Solis S4 is Meridian's 2024 all-rounder. It aims to do everything well
without a headline weakness, at a price that undercuts the flagships.

## Display

A 6.6-inch flat OLED at 1080 x 2400. It refreshes adaptively between 60Hz and
120Hz and reaches 2000 nits of peak brightness. The panel supports HDR10+ and
Dolby Vision.

## Camera

The rear system is a 50MP main sensor with optical stabilisation, a 12MP
ultrawide, and a 10MP 3x telephoto. It is consistent across lighting rather than
spectacular in any one area. Night mode stacks up to 12 frames. Video records at
4K 60fps.

## Battery and charging

A 5000mAh cell delivers up to 26 hours of video playback. Wired charging peaks
at 67W, filling the battery in about 45 minutes. Wireless charging tops out at
15W.

## Performance

Built on the upper-mid Corona 8 with 8GB of RAM, it stays smooth in most games
with only mild throttling in long sessions. Storage is 128GB or 256GB, not
expandable.

## Connectivity

It supports Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, and dual SIM plus eSIM. The USB-C port runs
at 5Gbps.

## Software and updates

A clean Android build with a handful of Meridian utilities. Meridian promises
three OS upgrades and four years of security patches.

## Price and availability

The Solis S4 launched at $699, in Midnight, Silver, and Coral. Sold unlocked and
through all major carriers.
